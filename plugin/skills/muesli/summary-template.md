# Meeting summary template

Use these headings in this order for every summary. Leave out a section that has nothing in it, except Decisions and Action items: write "None" there, so the reader knows it was checked. Write in the language of the meeting, plain and short.

```markdown
# {Meeting title}

**Date:** {date, start time} · **Length:** {minutes} min · **Source:** {live call or audio file}
**Participants:** {names, with role or company where it is known}

## Purpose
{One or two sentences: why the meeting took place and what it needed to settle.}

## Key points
- {Topic}: {what was said, in one to three sentences. Who raised it, when it matters.}
- {Topic}: ...

## Decisions
- {What was decided, by whom, and the reason when one was given.}

## Action items
| Owner | Action | Due |
|---|---|---|
| {Name} | {What they will do, specific enough to act on} | {Date or "not stated"} |

## Open questions
- {Question that was raised and not answered, and who was to find out.}

## Next steps
{When the group meets again or what happens next, if it was said.}
```

## Rules for filling it in

- **Order by topic, not by time.** Group everything about one subject, even when it came up in several places.
- **One line per decision and per action.** A decision states the outcome; an action has one owner and one task. Split an action that has two owners or two tasks.
- **Owners and dates only as stated.** Write "not stated" instead of guessing. When nobody took an item, list it under Open questions.
- **Numbers, names and dates exactly as the context fixes them.** Use the same spelling for a name or term everywhere.
- **Separate fact from opinion.** Put what was agreed under Decisions, and what one person suggested under Key points with that person's name.
- **Do not add what was not said.** No advice, no conclusions of your own and no filler. The summary should fit on one screen for a meeting of an hour.
- **Sensitive details** (salaries, health, personal matters) go in only when they are the point of the meeting.
